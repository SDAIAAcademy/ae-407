# Context and scope: Insaf

1. **The system and its limits.** Kind, purpose, outputs and their use. Intended uses. Uses out of scope. Users. People affected.
2. **Data, entries, exits, parties.** Data with classification level. Where content comes in. Where output goes. Everyone in the value chain.
3. **Automation and people.** Recommends, decides or acts. Where a person approves, reviews or stops. What is enforced and what is only intended. Responsibilities.
4. **Life cycle and change.** Stage. What changes, how often, who approves. What triggers a new assessment.
