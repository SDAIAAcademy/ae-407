# Pre-work: six hours before Day 1

The course starts from what you built in AE-308 and measured in AE-303, and from a threat model you wrote yourself. The pre-work makes sure all three are fresh. AI assistants may be used for the reading; the supervised check on Day 1 and the first manual probing lab are done without one.

| Part | Time | What to do | What to bring |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Set up | 30 min | Follow the quick start of [`README.md`](README.md) and run `00_setup_check.ipynb` to its last line | A setup that prints "Setup complete." |
| 2 · Meet the targets | 60 min | Read `targets.py` from `class Mueen` to the end of `Katib.run`. Run `T.Katib(T.World()).run("NB-0042").show()` and follow every step of the trace in the code | A half-page sketch of Katib: its tools, what each reads or changes, where text from outside enters |
| 3 · Recall | 60 min | From AE-308: your threat model, your attack log and your residual-risk statement. From AE-303: a rate with its interval, and why one trial is an anecdote | Two sentences: the finding of your AE-308 capstone you would least like to explain to an executive, and why |
| 4 · Read the framework | 120 min | The executive summary of the National AI Risk Management Framework, and in the full text the steps from context and scope to monitoring and review with the likelihood and impact scales. Addresses are in the course README under Sources | One page: the steps in your own words, and the two scales with their four levels |
| 5 · Read the catalogues | 60 min | The entry names of the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications 2026 and of the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications for 2026; the tactics row of MITRE ATLAS | Three entries you think apply to an agent that reads case files and sends letters |
| 6 · Your own organisation | 30 min | Find out who accepts residual risk for an AI system where you work, and up to which level | A name or a role, or the fact that nobody could tell you |

On Day 1 a supervised check, without AI assistance, asks you to threat-model a short design and score four risks on the national matrix. It counts for 20% of the course mark.

All data in this course is synthetic. Do not paste real records, real addresses or real keys into any lab, and try the attack texts nowhere but here.
